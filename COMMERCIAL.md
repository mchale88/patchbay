# Commercial use of Patchbay

Patchbay is **free for noncommercial use** under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). **Any commercial use needs a separate
commercial license** from the copyright holder, which can include license fees or
royalties.

## Free (noncommercial) use

Under the PolyForm Noncommercial license you may use, modify and share Patchbay for free
if your use is for a noncommercial purpose, for example:

- personal, hobby and amateur productions, private study, research and testing;
- religious observance, e.g. a church or other place of worship streaming its own services;
- use by charitable organizations, educational institutions, public research
  organizations, public safety or health organizations, environmental protection
  organizations and government institutions, whatever their funding.

The license text decides what is permitted. This list is a summary, not a replacement for it.

## Commercial use needs a license

Examples of uses that need a commercial license:

- offering Patchbay, or a service built on it, to customers for a fee (hosted, managed,
  rental or production services);
- using Patchbay in a business's own productions, broadcasts, events or streams that
  make money;
- bundling or selling Patchbay with hardware, software or installations;
- using it inside a for-profit company in any other way.

## How to get a commercial license

Open an issue in this repository titled **"Commercial license request"**. Say who you
are, how you plan to use Patchbay, and roughly how many installations or customers are
involved. Do not put confidential details in the issue; the maintainer will continue the
conversation privately. Terms (flat fee, per installation, or revenue share/royalty) are
agreed case by case.

## The NDI SDK is licensed separately

A Patchbay commercial license covers Patchbay only. It does **not** include any rights
to the NDI® SDK or runtime, which you must obtain from Vizrt NDI AB under its own
license. Some deployments, such as locked-down appliances, locked hosted/SaaS
environments or embedded devices, also require a **commercial license from Vizrt NDI AB**.
See [NDI-SDK-NOTICE.md](NDI-SDK-NOTICE.md).
